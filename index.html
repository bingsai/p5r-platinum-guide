Overview

Persona 5 Royal's Platinum takes one careful playthrough of about 80–100 hours, on any difficulty. The one thing that can wreck it is the calendar: miss the Councillor deadline and the Third Semester never happens, which locks out a trophy you need.

In this guide

  • Month-by-month roadmap with a checkbox for each phase
  • The three Confidant deadlines for the Third Semester and true ending
  • A day-by-day planner covering every in-game day from 4/9 to 2/3
  • Interactive trophy tracker with spoiler shield, filters and save backups
  • Every classroom and exam answer, searchable by date
  • Where to find every Confidant and how to rank them faster
  • Weaknesses and resistances for all 232 Personas and 129 named Shadows

Don't miss this

Get Councillor (Takuto Maruki) to Rank 9 before 11/18. Rank 10 is automatic. Miss it and the Third Semester, and with it the Platinum, is gone for this run.

Also strongly recommended: Justice (Akechi) Rank 8 by 11/17, and Faith (Kasumi) Rank 5 by 12/18.

PS5 DLC is already in your game

The PS5 version comes with nearly all previously released DLC. The cardboard box in Joker's attic (from 4/12) gives you 100,000+ yen, items, and overpowered DLC Personas you can summon for free. Claiming it is optional and can't be undone.

DLC Personas take up level slots in their Arcana, so a few standard Velvet Room fusion recipes shift slightly. If a guide's recipe gives you the wrong Persona, switch on the DLC option in your fusion calculator.

Thieves Den awards aren't trophies

Thieves Den awards pay out P Medals and are an in-game list, separate from PlayStation trophies. Many of them overlap with trophy activities, though, so ticking them off moves you toward the Platinum too.

    Daily Planner

    What to do with every in-game day, from the first morning to the final boss. It's built to hit every deadline and pick up trophies along the way. Tick off each day as you play it and the planner remembers where you are.

    How to use it

    • Each day has a daytime plan (after school, or all day on holidays) and an evening plan.
    • For every Confidant hangout, carry a Persona of their Arcana. It's assumed throughout.
    • Class and exam answers live in the Classroom Answers section.
    • Off by a day? Rank-up dates shift if you miss a point threshold. Do the next listed hangout and carry on. Only the red deadline days are fixed.

      Confidant Cheat Sheet

      Where to find everyone, who has a deadline, and two ways to rank up faster.

      Fortune: Affinity Reading

      • Unlocks at Chihaya Rank 5 and costs ¥5,000 in Shinjuku.
      • Pick a Confidant and Chihaya deepens your bond with them.
      • Use it on whoever is closest to ranking up, especially Maruki and Akechi before 11/17.

      Matching Arcana bonus

      • Carry a Persona of the same Arcana as the Confidant you're meeting for bonus points.
      • Higher-level Personas of that Arcana help more.
      • Fuse the right Arcana in the Velvet Room before a big hangout day.
