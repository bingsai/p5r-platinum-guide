PS5 DLC Note

The PS5 version comes with nearly all previously released DLC. The cardboard box in Joker's attic (available from 4/12) gives you 100,000+ yen, items, and overpowered DLC Personas that can be summoned for free. Claiming it is optional and can't be undone.

DLC Personas also take up level slots in their Arcana, so some standard Velvet Room fusion recipes shift slightly. If a guide's recipe gives you the wrong Persona, turn on the DLC toggle in your fusion calculator.

Thieves Den ≠ Trophies

Thieves Den awards (paid out in P Medals) are an in-game achievement list and are separate from PlayStation trophies. Many awards overlap with trophy activities, though, so working through them moves your Platinum forward too.

Confidant Cheat Sheet

Where to find everyone, who has a deadline, and how to rank up faster.

Fortune: Affinity Reading

  • Unlocks at Chihaya Rank 5 and costs ¥5,000 (Shinjuku).
  • Pick a Confidant and Chihaya deepens your bond with them, so your next hangout counts for more.
  • Best on whoever is closest to a rank-up, especially Maruki and Akechi before 11/17.

Matching Arcana Bonus

  • Carry a Persona of the same Arcana as the Confidant you're meeting for bonus points.
  • Higher-level Personas of that Arcana help more.
  • Visit the Velvet Room before a big hangout day to fuse the right Arcana.
